# The Fool's Journey

*A tarot adventure in 22 levels.*

You are the Fool, with a little white dog at your side, setting off from a cliff's edge to walk the whole of the Major Arcana. Each level belongs to one Major card. Every task, puzzle and conversation is built around what that card means, so by the end of the journey you've learned the deck by living it.

**▶ [Play the game](https://hmorfeld.github.io/fools-journey/)**

---

## How to play

- **Tap or click** anywhere on the map to walk there.
- **Tap a person or object** to talk to them or look at it.
- **Gold diamonds** mark something worth checking.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to talk.
- Your **journal** tracks tasks, and the **hints** section collects tips as you find them.
- Every level starts by meeting its Major. Talk to them first, then explore.

Progress saves automatically in your browser. Use **Restart this level** to replay the current level, or **Start over** to begin a brand-new journey.

**Levels 1–10** each ask for the four suit cards of their number: Aces at the Magician, Twos at the High Priestess, and so on up to the Tens at the Wheel of Fortune.

**Levels 11–14** each ask for one suit's four court cards (Page, Knight, Queen and King): Swords for Justice, Cups for the Hanged Man, Pentacles for Death and Wands for Temperance.

**Levels 15–20** turn the deck itself into the challenge. The Devil tempts you to trade your cards away, the Tower scatters them, the Moon hides them in fog, and Judgement asks you to remember where each one came from.

**Level 21, the World,** is the final test: a 22-question tarot quiz drawn at random from the whole deck. Miss the same card three times and you're sent back to its level to learn it again.

## Learning tarot

- Every card you earn comes with a short meaning, and you can see your whole collection in the side panel.
- After you finish, **Practice your tarot** lets you retake the quiz with 22 new cards, plus a study list of any cards you missed.

## Technical notes

- The whole game is a single file, `index.html`, with no installs, libraries or internet connection needed once it's loaded.
- It works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone.
- Saves are stored in your browser's local storage, so they stay on your device and browser. Clearing your browser data erases your save.
